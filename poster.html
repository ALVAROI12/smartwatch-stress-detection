Problem Statement

Chronic stress affects 75% of adults globally, contributing to cardiovascular disease, mental health disorders, and reduced quality of life.

Challenge: Traditional stress assessment relies on self-reports, which are subjective, delayed, and impractical for continuous monitoring.

Opportunity: Commercial smartwatches with PPG, EDA, and accelerometer sensors enable ubiquitous, objective stress detection.

Objective

Develop a robust, interpretable deep learning system for real-time stress detection using multi-modal physiological signals from consumer wearables.

  • Achieve high accuracy across diverse populations
  • Ensure model interpretability for clinical trust
  • Enable real-time deployment on edge devices
  • Validate across multiple independent datasets

Real-World Impact

  • Mental Health: Early intervention for anxiety and burnout through continuous passive monitoring
  • Workplace: Organizational stress analytics while preserving individual privacy
  • Healthcare: Remote patient monitoring for stress-related conditions
  • Research: Standardized framework for affective computing studies

Key Contributions

  • Multi-dataset fusion: First study combining WESAD, EPM-E4, and WDD-Stress with unified preprocessing
  • Hybrid architecture: Novel CNN-LSTM design optimized for wearable signal processing
  • Interpretable AI: SHAP-based explanations aligned with physiological stress markers
  • Edge deployment: Optimized model achieving <50ms inference on Apple Watch / WearOS
  • Open source: Full pipeline released with reproducible experiments
